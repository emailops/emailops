### New

- **Skills included with the app**: reviewed skills you can read in full and switch on one by one in **Skills**. They stay off until you switch them on, and **Edit a copy** makes one your own.
- **Skills are on by default** and no longer experimental. A skill runs only when you start a message with `/name`.
- **Search and filters list every match**, newest first, and load more as you scroll. They used to stop at 100 per account.
- **Adding an account syncs faster.**

### Fixes

- AI drafts are written in the language of the message they answer, and no longer sign twice.
- The chat reads open PDFs reliably.
- Drafts from Gmail and Outlook keep their attachments.
- Sync keeps running on networks that block the connectivity check.
- Images in older mail show again.
- Forwarding waits for the forwarded files, and **Discard** in a compose tab removes the draft.
