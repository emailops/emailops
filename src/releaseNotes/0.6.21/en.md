### New

- **Import .ics files**: Calendar → Import .ics previews the events in a file and adds them to your Google or Outlook calendar.
- **Answer invitations missing from your calendar**: answering from the invitation card adds the event and sends your answer to the organizer. The event detail can mail the participants and change Your response.
- **Delete forever**: on IMAP and Outlook accounts, delete the selected messages for good, or Empty Trash and Empty Spam.
- **Polish**: the composer edits the text you wrote as your instructions say, keeping its facts. The instructions are never sent.
- **Size and Has attachment columns**: choose them under Columns in the full-width list; the headers sort the whole list.
- **Your latest message first**: a new Conversation view in Settings → Appearance.
- **Attachments from All accounts**: one list and one set of rules for every account, with shared tags and sender suggestions.
- **Keep only the latest version of each file in a thread**: a new attachment rule option, on by default, also for the rules you already have.

### Privacy and security

- The update check now asks getemailops.com, with no identifier and no app version, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Check for new versions turns it off.
- Security updates for two libraries, and the OpenRouter connection no longer follows redirects.

### Fixes

- Outlook finds invitations organised outside Exchange, shows your own answer, and deletes a meeting you were invited to without an error.
- A click in the calendar lands on the event under the pointer.
- A chat reply no longer starts with text the AI threw away.
