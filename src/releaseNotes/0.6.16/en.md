### New

- **Dark theme**: Settings → Appearance → Theme chooses Light, Dark or System. Emails follow the dark theme whenever they can.
- **Not spam** moves a conversation out of Spam back to the Inbox, on your provider too.
- **All / Unread** toggle on email lists.
- **Font and font size** in the compose toolbar.
- **Test** a Lens's filters before saving: see how many emails match and the latest ones.
- **About** tab in Settings, with links to the website, the source code and the new sponsor page.

### Privacy and security

- IMAP on ports other than 993 (for example Proton Mail Bridge) upgrades to an encrypted connection and never continues in clear text.
- Security updates to the TLS and network libraries.

### Fixes

- AI drafts sign with your name instead of a "[Your name]" placeholder.
- Forwarding a conversation carries every message and attachment, not only the latest one.
- New Lens columns fill in for emails already extracted, and long values no longer take over the Lens table.
- IMAP sync is steadier: one session per sync and retries when connecting fails.
- Linux: dropdowns follow the theme, and the AppImage recovers from font crashes.
- A PDF tab keeps its scroll position when you switch tabs.
