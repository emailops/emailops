### New

- **Scheduled backups**: the new Settings → Data & Backups tab copies your local data and the database at the time you choose, and restores a local copy from there.
- **Word documents**: `.docx` attachments open in the app, and the chat can use them as context, like a PDF.
- **The chat reads the email you are writing**: ask it whether your draft answers every question, or for a better version to copy.
- **Drafts on IMAP accounts**: drafts another mail client saved on the server show up in Drafts.
- **Send new messages from**: choose the account new messages start from, in Settings → Appearance.
- **Undo archive and delete**: Off, 6, 10, 20 or 30 seconds, in Settings → Appearance.
- **Resizable columns and a compact sidebar**: drag the edge of the sidebar or the email list to resize them. Compact is the new sidebar density.
- **Paste files into a message**: files copied in Finder are attached. The composer also gains a text colour picker and Clear formatting.

### Privacy and security

- A forged sender is harder to pass off as someone else.
- A crafted message or attachment can no longer make the app overwork, and spreadsheet import now takes `.xlsx` only.
- Security updates for sending mail and for cleaning email HTML.

### Fixes

- Meeting reminders come only from your own calendars, not from ones shared with you.
- Dates in the email list follow your region.
- Opening a Gmail or Outlook draft no longer rewrites it on the provider.
- An empty category tab stays empty while a sync runs.
- List previews no longer show codes such as `&#39;`.
- Invitations and calendar feeds with a fixed-offset time zone show at the right time.
