### New

- **Chat reads the open PDF**: with a PDF open in a tab or selected in Attachments, the chat answers about it and offers Summarise this document, Key points, dates and amounts, and Ask about this document. Long PDFs are read in parts.
- **Zoom** in the PDF viewer.
- **Suggested requests** when you start a new chat.
- **Calendar feeds (ICS) for IMAP accounts**, for example Proton Calendar, and a calendar panel docked on the right.
- **All accounts** in the calendar, with a colour per account.
- **OpenAI-compatible** AI provider with your own server address.
- **Data location** in Settings: move the folder that holds your mail, attachments and models, for example to an external disk.
- **Star on GitHub** next to Give feedback.

### Privacy and security

- Chat and AI prompts handle the text of your emails more defensively.
- Security updates to the app's libraries.

### Fixes

- A message with only an attachment now sends.
- A file dropped on a message you are writing is attached instead of opening full screen.
- The block-sender dialog closes when you confirm and reads well in the dark theme.
- A checked email stays readable in the dark theme.
