title: 'Documentation'
description: 'Everything you need to install, set up and get the most out of EmailOps — the email client that runs AI on your own machine.'
---

EmailOps is a desktop email client with local AI built in. It syncs your Gmail, Outlook and
IMAP accounts into a database on your own machine, and runs the AI features — chat, reply
drafts, classification, semantic search — against that local copy. By default nothing is sent
to a server: the model runs inside the app.

EmailOps is free and open source (Apache-2.0). The source lives at
[github.com/emailops/emailops](https://github.com/emailops/emailops).
