# Beat sheet for an EmailOps short

Fill the right-hand column for the feature, then turn each row into a shot
in the storyboard. Times are what the Lens short used (43.6 s in total).

| # | Beat | Lens short (web-form contacts) | Screen | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **POV** hook: the situation, pain highlighted | "POV: tu web te manda **clientes nuevos** cada día" | inbox, the notifications outlined | 3.8 s |
| 2 | **ANTES**: the manual chore, field by field | "Copiar el email… el teléfono… la empresa… y así con **cada cliente.**" | one email, fields outlined in red with labels | 5.0 s |
| 3 | **DESPUÉS**: name the feature | "Una **Lente** de EmailOps · La IA local lee tus correos y rellena una tabla" | card | 2.6 s |
| 4 | Walkthrough, one action per caption | "Abres **Lentes**" · "Nueva lente, **con el chat**" · "Le pides lo que **quieres sacar**" · "La IA prepara la **Lente**" · "Email, teléfono, empresa: **Crear**" · "Y la **ejecutas**" | each click zoomed; the request typed letter by letter | 16 s |
| 5 | Payoff, split screen | "De 5 correos a **1 tabla**" · ANTES / DESPUÉS, con IA local | email on top, table filling below | 6.0 s |
| 6 | What happens next | "¿Llega otro cliente? Su fila aparece **sola.**" | a new row slides in, outlined "nuevo" | 4.4 s |
| 7 | Close | EmailOps · Cliente de correo libre con IA local · getemailops.com | end card | 3.8 s |

## Why this format

The developer compared three cuts of the same feature:

- **Card-by-card walkthrough** (97 s): hook, problem, solution, concept and
  section cards around a slow walkthrough. Clear, but long, and the cards
  read as slides.
- **Result first, numbered steps** (35 s): opens on the finished table
  ("5 clientes nuevos. 0 copiar y pegar."), then ① ② ③.
- **POV → antes → después** (43 s): picked. The viewer recognises their own
  situation first, feels the chore, then sees it vanish; the split screen
  makes the gain obvious without a word of explanation.

Feedback that shaped it: the click on the first menu must be visible (add a
shot for it, zoomed on the sidebar); label the payoff with what matters to
the viewer ("DESPUÉS, con IA local"); show the "new email → new row" claim as
an animation, not a sentence.

## What the research said (September 2026)

- Hook in the first 1–3 s; keep intros to 2–3 s.
  [Shorz](https://www.shorz.ai/blog/best-youtube-shorts-hooks-for-saas-creators)
- 30–60 s, one feature, open on the problem; "script to the runtime, then
  cut 20 %". [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/product-demo-best-practices)
- Design for sound off: captions carry the story, zooms and cursor effects
  direct attention, music sets the pace.
  [ngram](https://www.ngram.com/blog/product-demo-best-practices)
- Auto-zoom on the element being used, click effects, smooth transitions,
  fictional but realistic demo data.
  [AutoZoom](https://autozoom.app/blog/screen-recording-best-practices-for-saas-product-demos)
- Structure as specific outcome, proof, one-line call to action.
  [Shorz](https://www.shorz.ai/blog/best-youtube-shorts-hooks-for-saas-creators)
